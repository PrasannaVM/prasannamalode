Follow-the-Sun Shift Planner

Add the teams that share your on-call or operations rota, give each a local shift window, and see the whole day in UTC: who is on, where shifts overlap for handover, and where nobody is watching.

Offsets use each zone's current UTC offset as computed by your browser, so daylight saving is handled automatically for today's date.
