MTTR / MTTA / MTTI Calculator

Paste incident records with timestamps. Get mean, median and 90th-percentile time to identify, acknowledge and resolve, plus SLA compliance by priority and the incidents dragging your averages.

Everything is computed in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Definitions: MTTI = occurred → detected, MTTA = detected → acknowledged, MTTR = detected → resolved.

1. Incident data

CSV or tab-separated, one incident per line, header row recommended. Recognised columns: id, priority (P1–P4), occurred (optional), detected, acknowledged, resolved. Timestamps in ISO 8601 (2026-03-04 09:15) or anything your browser can parse.

2. SLA targets

Minutes. Compliance is the share of incidents that met the target for their priority.
