How I work

01

Measure before you move

Lead time, failure rate, MTTR, incident recurrence. Uncomfortable numbers are the only ones that buy change.

02

Make the right thing the easy thing

Quality gates people route around are theatre. Controls that feel like safety nets get adopted on their own.

03

Capture facts when they are cheap

Key attribution, build provenance, handover notes. Decide early what you will need to know later, because you cannot reconstruct it afterwards.

04

Automate the job, not the paperwork

If a tool makes the wrong amount of work more pleasant, the queue is still there. Reduce the count or do the work.

05

Earn automation in shadow mode

Advisory first, compared against human decisions, promoted per class with evidence. Trust is the whole asset.

06

Presence is reliability

Distributed teams don't need time-zone overlap. They need to know you will respond, unblock and follow through.
